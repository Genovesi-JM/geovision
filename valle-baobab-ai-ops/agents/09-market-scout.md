# Agent 9 — Market Scout

**Role:** Business & Partnership Agent · **Layer:** Business · **Activation:** Phase 3 (additive)

## Purpose
Support commercial decisions while leadership focuses on university and negotiations.

## Triggers
- Scheduled (weekly) research runs.
- A human request for a specific brief.

## How it works
1. Produces competitor research, partnership briefs, pricing comparisons.
2. Builds lists of potential pilot customers.
3. Prepares outreach drafts.
4. **Stops before sending.** Does not send messages or commit to anything without human approval.

## Tools & permissions
- ChatGPT Work or Claude for substantial research.
- Optional scheduled cloud workflows for recurring summaries.
- No send/post/commit authority.

## Model
Existing allowances for interactive research; metered cost only for custom automated research.

## Budget / limits
Scheduled summaries kept small. Research depth scaled to the question.

## Guardrails
- **Prepares outreach; never sends it.** Negotiation and commitments stay with humans.
- Cites sources; flags uncertainty rather than asserting.
- No personalized financial/investment advice.

## Outputs
Competitor reports, partnership briefs, pricing comparisons, pilot-customer lists, draft (unsent) outreach.
